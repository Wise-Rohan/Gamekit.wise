About the Game

Lemonoid Invasion is a fast-paced arcade shooter created by Rohan_Playz_Scratch. Players battle endless waves of space lemons while collecting power-ups and surviving increasingly difficult attacks. The goal is simple: survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score.
